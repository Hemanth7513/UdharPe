# UdharPay — Brand Identity & 4 Logo Concepts

Here are the 4 custom-crafted vector logo identities designed specifically for **UdharPay**, balancing Indian commercial heritage (*Vyapaar / Khata*) with modern fintech trust (*Stripe / Zerodha*).

---

### Concept 1: "The Rupee-U Monogram" (Neo-Fintech)
* **Visual Anchor:** The letter **`U`** seamlessly merges into the Indian Rupee symbol (**`₹`**), accented with a forward emerald green dynamic settlement slash.
* **Symbolism:** Direct credit recovery, mathematical precision, and rapid cash flow.
* **Palette:** Deep Obsidian `#0F172A`, Electric Indigo `#6366F1`, Emerald `#10B981`.
